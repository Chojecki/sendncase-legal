Privacy Policy – SEND'N'CASE (English)

Last updated: 20 October 2026

SEND'N'CASE ("the game") is made by Quiet Death Games ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your data when you play the game on iPhone.

Short version: we do not collect your personal data. The game has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no servers of ours.

What stays on your device

The game stores the following only on your device, so it can remember your progress:

  • your settings (language, camera, sound, controls),
  • your best times and style scores on each trail,
  • your bike setup, paint and riding school progress.

We never receive this data. Deleting the game removes it.

Game Center

The game uses Apple's Game Center for leaderboards. If you are signed in to Game Center, your best times and scores are sent to Apple together with your Game Center player name, and other players can see them on the leaderboards. This is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not receive your Apple ID, your real name or any other personal data. You can play without Game Center: the game then works offline and no scores are sent.

What we do not do

  • We do not use advertising, tracking or analytics services.
  • We do not use crash-reporting services.
  • We do not ask for access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location.
  • We do not sell or share personal data, because we do not collect any.

Children

The game does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we do not collect personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have any question about this policy, write to us at: mar.chojecki@gmail.com.

Changes

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example, a new online feature), we will update this policy and the date above before the change takes effect.
